Lumine is a cheery octoling from the splatlands, when she's not battling, she's hanging out with Cori or tinkering with all kinds of machinery.


Lumine was a scavenger before she was able to pick herself back up, taking routine trips around the crater to try find whatever octarian treasures she could to hopefully sell to people, she's always been interested in the machinery that they managed to produce.


On one of these trips searching for treasures, she found Cori passed out on the shore and went to see if she was alright, glimpsing her discoloured tentacles beneath her hood.
Since Lumine knew octarian (due to searching for their treasures) she could talk to Cori, and she offers her a place to stay for the night, it was also during the ride back that Cori was given a pair of goggles by Lumine to shield her eyes from the sand.


Lumine went through a time where she didn't have a place to belong, much like Cori. During this time, she found people who helped her through and she wishes to be there for Cori in the same way others were there for her.


She has feelings for Cori, and takes every opportunity she can to show her affection for her.
