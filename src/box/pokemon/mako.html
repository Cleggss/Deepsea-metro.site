FIRST THING I GOTTA GET OUT THE WAY yes he shares a name with my other oc Mako Im a little lazy with names but I think it suits them both,,, also I was hungry when I named them and thinking of seafood so

Mako is a shiny weavile I found in Orre, they were one of the first ribbon masters I decided to do alongside Ringo

I'll start this off by saying... the reason I really like weavile is honestly mostly cause of pokken tournament, you know that one dinky spinoff that most people only post about cause of the really good models and as a "why cant gamefreak..." yeah you get the idea
ANYWAY I really love the characterisation of weavile in this game, each pokemon is an individual one so you can actually get a defined personality and the weavile you play as belongs to Nia and is shown to be mischevious and lazy through his animations which are very cute aweee my sckrunklyyy awww

this is the reason why I really like weavile so much though this game sparked the obsession ^^;

I'd imagine Mako's personality to be similar to a rescue cat that was taken in... still very scruffy and cheeky but happy to accept food and the like, bringing gifts to his trainer (usually consisting of coins he "found" or shiny objects)

For pretty much the entire RM challenge Mako has swept the floor with pretty much every elite four/champion that there is,, ESPECIALLY cynthia, he managed to take on her entire team by himself and still won without being hit once, my glorious goat

At the moment I'm running Night slash, Brick break, Ice shard and Swords dance with him, I'll probably change his moveset when it comes time to do the master rank ribbon, but this has given me no issues so far.
