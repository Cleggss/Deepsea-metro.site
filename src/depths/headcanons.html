Sanitization

Probably one of the most important things, since a majority of my oc's lore is based around it

For official definition

"Sanitized Octarians have been sterilized of life's energies with blended ink created by Commander Tartar. Subjects who are chosen as candidates to aid in this ink's creation are tested beforehand through the Kamabo Co. underground research facilities; if successful, the tests conclude with Tartar tricking the subjects into being blended to help create the ink. The process of sanitizing Octarians with this ink results in them losing all memory and identity, and submitting to Kamabo Co.'s ideas. Marina notes that the sanitized Octarians appear to have no pulse or signs of life."

Anyway, for MY headcanons

Ok, sanitization is a very tricky thing to talk about given the fact it seems to have so many different effects, so i'll split this up a bit

Partial sanitization

Eight is said to be sanitized, but it only seems to have affected their memories, rather than their appearance, which leads me to the first thing i'd like to define, partial sanitization is a procedure where the subject's memories are extracted and reshaped into mem cakes, this is used as a strategy to get the subject to perform the best in their tests, with them working under the assumption that the promised land is real. this procedure invokes amnesia in the subject, which can be treated with returning the mem cakes belonging to them.
This typically has no permament effects, aside from a foggy memory around the time the subject was partially sanitized.

Complete sanitization

This is the main kind of sanitization, which carries the side effects of turning the subject green, Discolouring their tentacles and permanently altering their ink. The procedure aims to instill an unwaivering loyalty to Kamabo Co. in the subject, to remove any external doubts and feelings that may interfere with their work for the company, Cori underwent this procedure after failing the testing track due to the proficient marksmanship she showed, however, her procedure was mishandled, which lead to her glowing pupils
Many effects of this are permanent.

Anyway, for MY headcanons

I do think there's gonna be SOME different concentrations of ink, as eight doesn't get turned green IMMEDIATELY after being shot with ink, and is even shown as having residual left on them in promo art [pictured]
Rather, I think that it's generally a lower concentration used by footsoldiers, and the higher concentration stuff is used sparingly in cases like agent 3's mind control... which also makes me believe that sanitization takes some time to complete as tartar had no reason NOT to try and permanently sanitize 3, yet they were controlled for a time...

High concentration ink is also detrimental to ink production, since there is an increased strain being placed on the body, this concentrated ink would probably sting to touch aswell.
additionally, higher concentration ink is used by Cori in my oc lore, experimentation on her lead to her ink being permanently concentrated...
I also think that sanitized ink would be "incompatible" with regular ink applications if this makes sense?
it doesn't mix well, you can't have it change to team colours, stuck with the gradiented hair... Of course this only applies to complete sanitization though
