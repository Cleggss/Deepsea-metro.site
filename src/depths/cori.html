Cori

Cori is a sanitized Octoling who only 'escaped' from the metro years after everyone else left. She is reliant on Lumine, who found her passed out on a shore and offered her a place to stay, they've lived together ever since.

Despite this... Cori is still haunted by her actions, the time she spent in the metro has permanently affected her, she often finds herself lost, ruminating on both people and events in her past...
She only has vague memories of her time in the army, unbeknowest to her, she can still be traced back to her training due to her combat knowledge and behaviours

Sanitization

Cori's sanitization is unique, due to an improper sanitization and experiments performed on her during service, she has permanently concentrated ink which she produces less of in addition to her glowing white pupils and the unnatural gradient on her tentacles.

Ink

The ink is more acidic in comparison to regular sanitized ink while also appearing darker, it was created with the aim to dispatch targets much more efficiently, piercing through armour and leaving wounds with greater ease, these differences have resulted in it being incompatible with regular sanitized ink.

These alterations have lead to the ink being able to harm Cori after it leaves her body, she was heavily scarred by it after a Mission and as a result her right arm has been damaged greatly, she experiences muscle weakness in the affected area in addition to having a severe discolouring, with a marbled pattern where her E-liter rested.

Tentacles

The colouring of cori's tentacles were due to the various experiments on her ink, which have left her with an unnatural gradient as well as a glowing pattern.

The length of cori's tentacles were due to her being left in kamabo's storage for an extended period of time, however, the tentacles curling depending on her emotion is entirely subconscious (as if like blushing!), as she cannot exert any control over them otherwise.

Eyes

Her eyes resemble the octarian soldiers more than other sanitized octolings, and her glowing white pupils were caused by the initial improper sanitization she underwent.


Due to all of these factors, she feels extremely distant from anyone else, the fact her ink was specifically engineered to harm people makes her consider herself a danger to others, and she tries not to get involved with anyone as a result.
